# Titan BOS — Best Selling Features

## The One-Line Pitch
**"The only AI operating system built exclusively for cleaning businesses. One system. Zero BS."**

## 1. Built for Cleaning — Not Adapted From Generic Software
Most job management tools are generic field-service platforms bolted onto cleaning. Titan BOS is purpose-built for the niche. Pre-loaded workflows for Residential, Bond, Pressure Washing, and Solar Panel cleaning. Niche terminology — technicians see *bond schedule*, *pre-clean walkthrough*, *evidence chain* — not generic "job types". Compliance logic baked in: bond schedule adherence, real estate handoff requirements, electrical safety for solar, damage risk assessment for pressure washing. Auto-generates Bond Packs, inspection reports, SWMS, and handover documents — no manual templates.

## 2. AI That Actually Runs the Business (Titan Zero)
Not an AI button on a dashboard. An operational intelligence engine. Procedural memory remembers how your company works and applies it to every job. Inspection strategy advisor predicts what real estate agents and inspectors will check, pre-arms the crew. Training gap detector identifies skill gaps before they cause a failed job. Artefact auto-composer turns photos, checklists, and notes into a complete bond pack automatically. Policy translator converts compliance standards into executable checklist logic. Conversational control lets operators instruct the system in plain language — the AI plans, proposes, and executes.

## 3. Nine Coordinated Apps — One Business Truth
Not nine separate tools that don't talk to each other. One operational record, nine role-specific surfaces. **Titan Go** (field tech) — offline-first guided execution and evidence capture. **Ground Zero** (dispatch ops) — lifecycle-aware scheduling and constraint AI. **Titan Pro** (owner/manager) — margin heatmaps, intervention radar, compliance index. **Titan Solo** (independent operator) — daily route optimiser and income stability predictor. **Titan Hello** (front-of-house) — multi-channel booking AI and intent detection. **Titan Zero** (AI core) — institutional memory, checklists, artefacts. **ZeroPay** (payments) — on-site collection and zero-fee-first rails. **Zero Fuss** (client portal) — evidence delivery and expectation alignment. **Titan Studio** (growth) — review automation, upsell detection, local ads. Technicians clean instead of retyping. Real-time truth everywhere.

## 4. Offline-First Field Execution
Titan Go runs fully offline — works in basements, rural sites, and low-signal buildings. Jobs, checklists, evidence, and signatures sync when connectivity returns. No lost data. Legal-grade evidence chain created locally. Completion confidence score predicts inspection pass probability before the crew leaves the site.

## 5. Evidence Chain That Kills Disputes
Bond cleaning disputes are a multi-million dollar pain point for operators. Automatic before/after photo galleries with timestamps and GPS. Offline evidence chain creates legally defensible documentation. Bond pack auto-generation for real estate handoffs. Evidence confidence engine requests photo retakes automatically if quality is poor.

## 6. Smart Dispatch — Not Just a Calendar
Ground Zero's lifecycle orchestration vs. calendar-slot scheduling. Delay cascade simulation predicts downstream impacts before they happen. Constraint-aware AI optimises across travel time, equipment availability, weather, and SLA deadlines simultaneously. Dispatch risk meter warns operators of likely failures before they occur. Zone-based work mapping for large commercial and construction cleans.

## 7. The Zero Philosophy — No Hidden Costs, No Lock-In
Six concrete promises that differentiate from competitors. **Zero Hidden Pricing** — no per-user seats, no per-API token charges, no surprise overages. **Zero AI Lock-In** — bring your own API keys, use Claude, OpenAI, Groq, or local models. **Zero Data Resale** — your data is never trained on, sold, or harvested. **Zero Workflow Duplication** — one operational record, no retyping jobs across apps. **Zero Learning Curve** — niche terminology loads for your service mode. **Zero Platform Dependency** — your processes are portable, Titan serves you. Competitor contrast: Jobber charges per user. ServiceM8 takes a cut. Swept is US-centric. Titan charges flat — and the AI is yours.

## 8. Multi-Channel Communications Built In
No add-on needed for comms. Titan Hello handles SMS, email, WhatsApp, voice, and portal with unified conversation memory. Intent detection distinguishes quotes, urgent work, renewals, and complaints automatically. Smart rebooking engine detects renewal moments and prompts the right follow-up. 15–20% higher rebooking rate from automated follow-up intelligence.

## 9. Payments — On the Doorstep
ZeroPay closes the gap between job completion and money in the bank. QR codes and payment links via email, SMS, WhatsApp, portal, and printed invoices. Zero-fee-first rails promote PayID, bank transfer, and cash to protect margins. On-site collection via the Titan Go app immediately at job completion. AI follow-up and voice payment recovery for overdue invoices.

## 10. Bring Your Own AI Keys — True Cost Control
A hard commercial advantage over competitors. Use your own Anthropic, OpenAI, or Groq API keys with no markup. Or use a local model for sensitive data and zero cloud cost. Per-tenant API key management — each client can use their own keys. Model routing uses a fast cheap model for suggestions and a capable model for complex planning.

## The Core Story Arc
**"Hello Receives. Zero Thinks. Titan Executes."** A customer books a bond clean. Titan Hello confirms, collects the property profile, and flags complexity. Titan Zero generates the checklist, SWMS, and pre-brief. Titan Go loads it on the tech's phone — offline-ready. Ground Zero tracks execution. Titan Pro monitors quality risk. ZeroPay collects on the doorstep. Zero Fuss delivers the evidence pack to the property manager. Everything connected. One business. One system. No duplication.
