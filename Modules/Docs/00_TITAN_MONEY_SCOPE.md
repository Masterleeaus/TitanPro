# Titan Money scope

Titan Money owns invoicing, accounting, receivables, statements, GST/compliance reporting, and late-invoice follow-up.

It does not execute payments. ZeroPay owns payment rails, checkout, PayID/card/bank/cash collection, QR payment sessions, gateway webhooks, wallets, and settlements. Titan Money may prepare handoff payloads for ZeroPay and consume ZeroPay payment-status signals.
